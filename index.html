The Dry Line

A run down an old toll railway. Up to four players: the keyboard counts as one device, every controller as another.

Players
On the title screen the first device to press a button (keyboard or any controller) is player 1. At the Junction any other device joins as the next player by pressing Jump. Hold Pause for one second at the Junction to leave. If a controller is unplugged the game pauses and names the player; reconnect it, or press a button on another device to take over.
Remap
Pause, Controls: choose an action, press Jump, press the new key or button. A key or button already in use is refused. The keys listed here are the defaults; the prompts in the game show your own.

How a run works

  • Every gate at the Junction is open from the start. Each line ends at a Warden.
  • The party carries three tokens in all (four with four players). Each token has a cut: pierce, arc, burst or ground.
  • At the start of a run every Warden gets a lock chart: three of the four cuts have a fare slot there, the fourth has none. The board in the Junction, the map (Tab) and each gate show the chart.
  • A charted fare slot opens only for a cut you carry when the fight starts. Three token hits punch a slot: the Warden loses 10% of its health, staggers and loses an attack. That token is then void.
  • Each extra player adds 60% to a Warden's health. Every Warden can be beaten with slashes alone.
  • Every fight is graded: Beaten, Par (faster than the Warden's par time) and Clean (nobody was hit). A Clean fight gives one punched token back: into a free slot, or onto the arena floor. Grades show on the map and the end screens.
  • The Long Clock borrows one attack from each Warden still alive: the Terminus gate and the map say which. Beat the Wardens first and it fights with its own attacks only; go straight there and it is the hardest road.
  • Each line's guarded plinth needs the move found on the previous line (Dash opens Signal Reef's, Wall Grip Salt Cutting's, Double Jump Ticket Hall's, Heavy Cut Wreck Quay's); the doorway and the map say which.
  • Trials hold signal posts that teach new moves. Tokens come from four guarded plinths (one per line), two lost trunks (they open when the room is clear) and one ticket machine (30 scrip, then 45). The kiosk sells trinkets only.
  • A standpipe in the Junction and one before the last Warden each mend the party once. A Warden gives one more health pip, 40 scrip and a choice of one of three trinkets.
  • Punching a slot of a cut for the first time unlocks that cut's rare tokens for every later run. A run code (pause or run-over screen) replays the same world, chart and rewards: add it to the address as #CODE.
