Tollkeeper

Movement and boss prototype. Click the game, then play with a keyboard or a controller.

Controls

Move
Arrows or W A S D
Jump
Z or Space. Hold for height.
Slash
X or J. Hold Up or Down to aim.
Dash
C, K or Shift
Use item
V or L
Mend
Hold F on the ground
Take item
Down at a pedestal
Controller
A jump, X slash, B dash, Y item, LB mend

How the fight works

  • The boss opens a weak point only for an item you carry.
  • Ember Lance flies straight. It cracks the eye on the face.
  • Arc Flask is lobbed. It cracks the hump when it lands from above.
  • Tremor Bell pulses around you. It cracks the belly when the boss rears or leaps.
  • Three hits break a weak point: big damage, a stagger, and the boss loses an attack. The item is then spent.
